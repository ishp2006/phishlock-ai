Defensive Architecture

Phishing Countermeasures & Defensive Hygiene

Structured security principles for recognizing deceptive hostnames, intercepting social engineering attacks, and executing rapid containment.

URI Forensics

Inspect Canonical Root Hostnames

Adversaries stack subdomains to simulate trusted infrastructure, e.g., chase.com.security-verify.xyz. The authoritative domain is security-verify.xyz. Parse backwards from the first single path delimiter (/) to verify the registered root.

Untrusted: paypal.login-update.top Legitimate: paypal.com/signin
Social Engineering

Analyze Coercive Urgency & Pressure

Social engineering campaigns rely on psychological pressure to bypass critical verification. Legitimate financial institutions do not require emergency wire transfers or identity re-verification under threat of immediate termination.

Protocol: Treat unprompted communications with arbitrary deadlines as untrusted until verified via out-of-band channels.
Defensive Controls

Deploy FIDO2 WebAuthn & Passkeys

SMS and standard time-based one-time password (TOTP) codes can be intercepted by adversary-in-the-middle (AiTM) reverse proxies. Hardware security keys and Passkeys are cryptographically bound to the genuine origin, neutralizing phishing proxies.

Zero-Trust Benefit: Hardware keys refuse to exchange cryptographic credentials with lookalike domains.
Incident Response

Containment & Compromise Recovery

1. Isolate: Sever network connectivity if malicious payloads or browser extensions were downloaded.
2. Rotate: Invalidate compromised credentials from an isolated, clean endpoint.
3. Revoke: Terminate active OAuth sessions, refresh tokens, and authentication cookies.
4. Escalate: Submit full email headers and network artifacts to the Security Operations Center (SOC).
